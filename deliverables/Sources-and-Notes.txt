ABANDONED BUT NOT FORGOTTEN — PRESENTATION NOTES

The PowerPoint is a 9-slide project overview. The screenshots in the deck were supplied by the project owner and show the application UI.

Project status reflected in this presentation: 143 records across Earth (23), Moon (82), Mars (18), and Venus (20); 93 unique source pages; 102 coordinate records cross-checked against ingested source tables.

Color legend: Abandoned #FF2222; Launch #FFB057; Landing #4FC3FF; Facility #5FE7D8; Active rover #7DFFA8.

Important framing: this is an independent educational project built around the NASA Space Apps Challenge theme. It is not an official NASA product or endorsement.
